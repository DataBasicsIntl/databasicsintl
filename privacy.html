Privacy Policy

Data Basics International, Inc.

Last updated: October 2026

This policy applies to Data Basics International, Inc. ("we," "us") and our website, DataBasicsIntl.com (the "Site"). It explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company, the details of your message, and any files you upload or send us.
  • Information needed for an engagement: business information and documents you share with us so we can advise or assist you.
  • Technical information: our website host and service providers may automatically log basic technical data, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the Site running.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your messages and enquiries
  • To prepare proposals and deliver the services you engage us for
  • To communicate with you about your engagement
  • To protect the Site and our business from fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the files you send us for any purpose other than responding to you or performing our work for you.

Who we share it with

  • Our consultants: team members who need the information to respond to you or work on your engagement, all bound by confidentiality.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run the Site and our business, including GitHub (website hosting), HostGator (processing our online contact form and delivering it to us by email), Google Fonts (website fonts), and Microsoft (email). They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • When required by law: or to protect our rights, property or safety, or those of others.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and files only as long as needed to respond to you and handle any follow-up, and for the duration of any engagement, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Messages sent through our online form are delivered to us by email and are not stored on the website.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections on the Site and limiting access to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send highly sensitive information, such as passwords, Social Security numbers or bank or credit card numbers, through our online form.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@DataBasicsIntl.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them as required.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date.
