Our Services

Technology leadership and expertise

From strategic advice for CIOs to deep, hands-on technical work, our services are as varied as the executives and managers we work with.

Interim & Fractional Technology Leadership

There are times when IT departments need experienced executive support. Data Basics International's experienced CIO, CTO and CISO resources are available on an interim, fractional or activity basis. This is ideal for:

  • Start-ups that need to take their IT to the next level
  • Technology optimization
  • New solution implementation
  • Filling organizational gaps for an interim period of time
  • Smaller organizations where a fractional technology executive might be more appropriate
  • Leading the technology components of M&A carve-out and integration activities

Technology Due Diligence

During merger and acquisition opportunities, carve-out and divestiture activities, or investment-related transactions, it is prudent to have the technology underpinnings of the target organization well vetted and understood.

Data Basics International has been assisting clients with technology due diligence for over 30 years. We have given assurance that the technical foundations are sound, that the code is as it appears, that the offloading of technology will not hamper future efforts, and that the solutions in place are up to the task at hand and scalable for the future.
