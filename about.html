About Us

Experts and expertise, where they are needed

For over 30 years, Data Basics International has been helping companies realize business goals and strategies through the effective use of technology.

Our story

Founded on deep technical expertise

Data Basics International, Inc. was founded in the early 1990s to provide the market with the highest-caliber Informix database professionals.

We quickly expanded our expertise and our reputation to become a leading provider of database professionals: architects, administrators and engineers who could design and tune systems to meet any requirement.

Data Basics International grew as our clients' needs became more widespread. When CIOs and IT directors needed deep technical expertise in databases, operating systems, infrastructure, security, cloud, integration and other areas, they knew to call Data Basics International.

Today

Our current focus stays close to our roots

Providing experts and expertise where they are needed.

CIO Advisors

Experienced professionals, many of whom have served on both sides of the desk: as IT executives, and as consultants and advisors to CIOs across industries and geographies.

Enterprise Architects

Skilled EA resources who have mastered the art and science of enterprise architecture across the technical, organizational, process and political realms.

Technical Experts

And of course, our heritage business continues to provide the experts and expertise needed by IT departments around the globe.
