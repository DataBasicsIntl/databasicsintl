About Us

Experts and expertise, where they are needed

For over 30 years, Data Basics International has been helping companies realize business goals and strategies through the effective use of technology.

Our story

Founded on deep technical expertise

Data Basics International, Inc. was founded in the early 1990s to provide the market with the highest-caliber Informix database professionals.

We quickly expanded our expertise and our reputation to become a leading provider of database professionals: architects, administrators and engineers who could design and tune systems to meet any requirement.

Data Basics International grew as our clients' needs became more widespread. When CIOs and IT directors needed deep technical expertise in databases, operating systems, infrastructure, security, cloud, integration and other areas, they knew to call Data Basics International.

Today

Our technology roots with a passion for the business of IT

In conjunction with the Boardroom and with the C-Suite, our work today is focused on maximizing the business value of IT. For some of our clients, that means helping to make the most of AI. For others it is making the IT organization more efficient and effective. Our PE, VC, and Portco clients call on us to help them understand the value and risks of the IT they are acquiring, then helping them to make sure the tech best serves the new business. No matter where you require help making the most of the IT you have, or the IT you need, Data Basics International is here to make it so.

Technology Leadership

Experienced professionals, who have served on both sides of the desk: as IT executives, as practitioners and operators, and as consultants and advisors to CIOs across industries and geographies. Our experienced CIO / CTO / CISO leaders are able to step in and start making a difference.

Technology Due Diligence

Detail-oriented analysis to make sure you understand the technology, and the technology risks, you are considering. Assessments done in plain English. Numbers that reflect reality.

AI & Technology Experts

Of course, our heritage as a deep technology provider means that we continue to provide the experts and expertise needed by businesses around the globe. Infrastructure. Cloud. ERP. AI. We have the experience and the expertise to bring it all together for the benefit of your business.
